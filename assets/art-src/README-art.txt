LIFT art, SVG

All text is converted to outlines, so no fonts are needed. Shading uses SVG gradients and
filters (drop shadows, molding grain, cloth fiber). These render in browsers, Figma, Inkscape
and Affinity. Illustrator flattens some filter effects on import.

LIFT-panel.svg              Full face, default state (TAPE screen, starting patch), 1400 x 920
LIFT-panel-no-cables.svg    Same face with no cables
case/                       Blank case with the three recessed panels
screen/                     The screen in its frame for each mode: tape, synth, drum, mix, in, bay
knobs/                      The four knobs pointing straight up, with and without the tick ring
jacks/                      OUT (red) and IN (blue) patch points
plugs/                      Cable plugs in six colors, closed cap and stackable (open jack on top)
cables/                     A sample cable in six colors, closed and stackable
pads/                       Pad caps: off and lit (yellow, red, blue, black), plus the full 24-pad grid
membrane/                   The membrane keypad, each key, and blank cyan and red keys
keyboard/                   The two-octave keyboard, a natural key (plain and C), and a sharp key
brand/                      LIFT wordmark, alone and on the case color
symbols/                    Signal shapes (pitch, gate, mod, audio, clock) and the four knob glyphs
